The Why 01 / 06

Climate has a coordination problem.
Great products exist. Smart people exist.
Capital is moving. But the dots
aren't connecting.
We fix that.

No Fluff.

We're not a conference circuit or a LinkedIn group. Every interaction is oriented toward one thing: accelerating climate solutions that work commercially and at scale.

Cross-Pollinate.

The breakthroughs happen at intersections. When a clean energy startup meets a distribution network, when a policy expert connects with a product team — that's when things move.

Business First.

Climate action that isn't commercially viable doesn't scale. We back solutions that make business sense, create real value, and pull capital — not just grant money — into the sector.

How It Works 02 / 06

Cross-Engagement
as a Model

Most communities silo people by role or sector. We deliberately don't. The Climate Folks operates as a living network — where a clean tech founder, a corporate sustainability head, a climate fund manager, and a B2B SaaS tool all sit in the same room.

The leverage comes from friction between different types: a product finding its channel, a service finding its customer, a person finding their co-founder, a network finding its cause.

01

You bring your actual work to the table

No elevator pitches. No performative ESG. Tell us what you're building, what you need, and where you're stuck.

02

The network maps your leverage points

We actively connect you across products, services, people, and networks — not just passively introduce you to "someone interesting."

03

Collaboration is structured, not accidental

Curated working groups, deal rooms, co-development sprints, and sector taskforces — built around problems, not personalities.

04

Impact compounds across the network

Every successful collaboration strengthens the ecosystem. Your win creates a case study, a relationship, and often a new product or partnership for someone else.
